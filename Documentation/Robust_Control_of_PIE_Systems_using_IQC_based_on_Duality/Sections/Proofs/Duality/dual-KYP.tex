\subsection{Proof of Corollary~\ref{thm:dual-KYP}}\label{proof:dual-KYP}
The last row of the primal component block gives the PI coordinate map
\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
\mcl M_\Gamma
&:=\bmat{
I&0&0\\
0&I&0\\
\mcl D_{21}\mcl C_{\mrm{G}}&\mcl C_2
&\mcl D_{21}\mcl D_{\mrm{G}}+\mcl D_{22}
},
&
\mcl M_{\Gamma^{-1}}
&:=\bmat{
I&0&0\\
0&I&0\\
-\mcl{\Lambda}\mcl D_{21}\mcl C_{\mrm{G}}&-\mcl{\Lambda}\mcl C_2&\mcl{\Lambda}
}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}
Both maps belong to $\Pi_4$, and direct block multiplication verifies that
they are inverses. In particular,
\begin{equation*}
\bmat{\mbf x_{\mrm{G}}\\\mbf x_\Theta\\\tilde w}
=\mcl M_\Gamma
\bmat{\mbf x_{\mrm{G}}\\\mbf x_\Theta\\w},
\qquad
\bmat{\mbf x_{\mrm{G}}\\\mbf x_\Theta\\w}
=\mcl M_{\Gamma^{-1}}
\bmat{\mbf x_{\mrm{G}}\\\mbf x_\Theta\\\tilde w}.
\end{equation*}
Thus $\mcl M_\Gamma$ is the PI congruence on the augmented
state--input coordinates. The causal map $\Gamma:w\mapsto\tilde w$ in
Theorem~\ref{thm:dual-iqc} is its induced input--output map. The construction
in the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:dual-iqc} shows that the corresponding coordinate map from
the dual component block is then invertible as well.

The primal and dual LPIs are the KYP inequalities for their respective
component blocks. Integration with the nonnegative storage gives statements~2
and~3 of Theorem~\ref{thm:dual-iqc}, respectively. Since these are equivalent,
either LPI yields
the strict primal hard IQC~\eqref{eq:robust-condition}.
Theorem~\ref{thm:robust-stability-performance}
then gives robust stability and robust performance.
