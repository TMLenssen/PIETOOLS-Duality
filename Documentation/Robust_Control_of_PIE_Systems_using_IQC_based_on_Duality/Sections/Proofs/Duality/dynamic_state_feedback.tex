\subsection{Proof of Corollary~\ref{thm:state-feedback}}\label{proof:state-feedback}
Since $\mcl P\succ0$, define $\mcl K_{\mrm{G}}$ and $\mcl K_\Theta$ by
\[
\mcl Z^*
=
\bmat{\mcl K_{\mrm{G}}&\mcl K_\Theta}\mcl P.
\]
The feedback-dependent rows of the dual component block give
\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
\underline{\mcl A}
&:=
\underline{\mcl A}_0
+
\bmat{\mcl K_{\mrm{G}}^*\\\mcl K_\Theta^*}
\bmat{\mcl B_{\mrm{u}}^*&0},
&
\underline{\mcl B}
&:=
\underline{\mcl B}_0
+
\bmat{\mcl K_{\mrm{G}}^*\\\mcl K_\Theta^*}
\mcl D_{\mrm{zu}}^* .
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}
Then \eqref{eq:controller-kyp} is the dual KYP inequality for
the closed-loop filtered system
$\mbf D(\Theta)\bmat{(K\star G)^\top\\I}$. From the last row of the
primal component block, its
PI congruence transformation and inverse are
\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
\mcl M_\Gamma
&:=\bmat{
I&0&0\\
0&I&0\\
\mcl D_{21}(\mcl C_{\mrm{G}}+\mcl D_{\mrm{zu}}\mcl K_{\mrm{G}})
&\mcl C_2+\mcl D_{21}\mcl D_{\mrm{zu}}\mcl K_\Theta
&\mcl D_{21}\mcl D_{\mrm{G}}+\mcl D_{22}
},\\
\mcl M_{\Gamma^{-1}}
&:=\bmat{
I&0&0\\
0&I&0\\
-\mcl{\Lambda}\mcl D_{21}(\mcl C_{\mrm{G}}+\mcl D_{\mrm{zu}}\mcl K_{\mrm{G}})
&-\mcl{\Lambda}(\mcl C_2+\mcl D_{21}\mcl D_{\mrm{zu}}\mcl K_\Theta)
&\mcl{\Lambda}
}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}
Equivalently, the primal filtered-input row is
\begin{equation*}
\bmat{\mbf x_{\mrm{G}}\\\mbf x_\Theta\\\tilde w}
=\mcl M_\Gamma
\bmat{\mbf x_{\mrm{G}}\\\mbf x_\Theta\\w}.
\end{equation*}
The last row of the dual component block gives the differently structured
dual map. By the primal--dual invertibility equivalence in
Theorem~\ref{thm:dual-iqc},
\begin{equation*}
\underline{\mcl{\Lambda}}
:=\bigl(\underline{\mcl D}_{21}\mcl D_{\mrm{G}}^*
+\underline{\mcl D}_{22}\bigr)^{-1}\in\Pi_4,
\end{equation*}
and
\begin{equation*}\
\mcl M_{\underline\Gamma}
:=\bmat{
I&0&0\\
0&I&0\\
\underline{\mcl D}_{21}\mcl B_{\mrm{G}}^*
&\underline{\mcl C}_2
&\underline{\mcl D}_{21}\mcl D_{\mrm{G}}^*+\underline{\mcl D}_{22}
},\quad
\mcl M_{\underline\Gamma^{-1}}
:=\bmat{
I&0&0\\
0&I&0\\
-\underline{\mcl{\Lambda}}\underline{\mcl D}_{21}\mcl B_{\mrm{G}}^*
&-\underline{\mcl{\Lambda}}\underline{\mcl C}_2
&\underline{\mcl{\Lambda}}
}.
\end{equation*}
That row acts as
\begin{equation*}
\bmat{\underline{\mbf x}_{\mrm{G}}\\\underline{\mbf x}_\Theta\\
\tilde{\underline w}}
=\mcl M_{\underline\Gamma}
\bmat{\underline{\mbf x}_{\mrm{G}}\\\underline{\mbf x}_\Theta\\\underline w}.
\end{equation*}
Unlike $\mcl M_\Gamma$, the dual map has no explicit controller-gain blocks.
The gains enter the dual state and input operators above. The analysis map
is recovered by setting
$\mcl K_{\mrm{G}}=\mcl K_\Theta=0$. These PI matrices act on the augmented
state--input coordinates and induce the causal maps $\Gamma$ and
$\underline\Gamma$ used in Theorem~\ref{thm:dual-iqc}. They are not those
input--output maps themselves. All four coordinate maps lie in $\Pi_4$.
Hence the dual KYP
inequality gives statement~3 of Theorem~\ref{thm:dual-iqc}, which is
equivalent to the strict primal hard IQC in statement~2. Therefore
Theorem~\ref{thm:robust-stability-performance} proves robust stability and
robust performance of $P_K\star\Delta$.
