\subsection{Proof of Class~\ref{class:sector-bounded-infinite-dimensional}}
\label{proof:sector-bounded-infinite-dimensional}
We use the multiplicative property of the dual operation such that $\mbf{D}(\Theta_1\Theta_2)=\mbf D(\Theta_1)\mbf D(\Theta_2)$
for any two invertible $\Theta_1$ and $\Theta_2$. Since $S_0\succeq 0$, $\mcl{S}$ is invertible with ${S_0}^{-1}\succeq 0$.
Applying this property to the two factors of $\Theta$ in
Class~\ref{class:sector-bounded-infinite-dimensional} gives
\begin{align*}
\mbf D(\Theta)
&=\mbf D(
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
\bmat{I&\mcl S\\I&-\mcl S}
)
\mbf D(
\bmat{\beta I&-I\\-\alpha I&I}
)\\
&=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}(\beta-\alpha)}
\bmat{-\mcl S^{-1}&-I\\-\mcl S^{-1}&I}
\bmat{\beta I&-I\\-\alpha I&I}.
\end{align*}
Therefore,
\begin{align*}
&\mbf D(\Theta)^*J_{\mbf n,\mbf n}\mbf D(\Theta)\\
&=\frac{1}{2(\beta-\alpha)^2}
\bmat{\beta I&-I\\-\alpha I&I}^*
\bmat{-\mcl S^{-1}&-\mcl S^{-1}\\-I&I}
J_{\mbf n,\mbf n}
\bmat{-\mcl S^{-1}&-I\\-\mcl S^{-1}&I}
\bmat{\beta I&-I\\-\alpha I&I}\\
&=\frac{1}{(\beta-\alpha)^2}
\bmat{\beta I&-I\\-\alpha I&I}^*
\bmat{0&\mcl S^{-1}\\\mcl S^{-1}&0}
\bmat{\beta I&-I\\-\alpha I&I}
=\underline{\mcl V}_\Delta,
\end{align*}
where the final equality substitutes
$\underline{\mcl S}=\mcl S^{-1}$.
