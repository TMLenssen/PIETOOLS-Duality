The IQC certificate of Section~\ref{sec:iqc-theory} becomes a controller
analysis condition once the state-feedback law is fixed. We examine the
primal control scheme shown in
Figure~\ref{fig:primal-controller-interconnection}, which corresponds to the
primal component block in Figure~\ref{fig:primal-system-components}. Starting
from this interconnection, we derive its filtered closed-loop realization and
identify the operator products that prevent a direct convex synthesis
formulation.
\primalcontrollerfigure
\noindent To distinguish analysis from synthesis, fix the IQC filter $\Psi$ and write an
augmented realization before closing the controller as
\begin{equation}\label{eq:primal-filtered-open-loop}
\bmat{\mcl T\dot{\mbf x}(t)\\\tilde v(t)}
=
\bmat{\mcl A&\mcl B&\mcl B_{\mrm u}\\
       \mcl C&\mcl D&\mcl D_{\mrm{zu}}}
\bmat{\mbf x(t)\\w(t)\\u(t)}.
\end{equation}
Under the signal-availability condition
in~(\ref{ass:signal-availability}), let $u(t)=\mcl K\mbf x(t)$, where
$\mcl K=\bmat{\mcl K_{\mrm P}&\mcl K_\Theta}$, the filtered graph
$\Psi\bmat{K\star G\\I}$ has the PIE realization
\[
\{\mcl T,\mcl A+\mcl B_{\mrm u}\mcl K,\mcl B,
\mcl C+\mcl D_{\mrm{zu}}\mcl K,\mcl D\}.
\]
A sufficient condition for robust stability and performance, \cite{lenssen2025muanalysissynthesisframeworkpartial},
is the existence of a PI operator $\mcl P=\mcl P^*\succeq0$ and $\epsilon>0$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:primal-controller-analysis}
\begin{gathered}
\bmat{\mcl T^*\mcl P(\mcl A+\mcl B_{\mrm u}\mcl K)
+(\mcl A+\mcl B_{\mrm u}\mcl K)^*\mcl P\mcl T
&\mcl T^*\mcl P\mcl B\\
\mcl B^*\mcl P\mcl T&\epsilon I}\\
+\bmat{(\mcl C+\mcl D_{\mrm{zu}}\mcl K)^*\\\mcl D^*}
\mcl V
\bmat{(\mcl C+\mcl D_{\mrm{zu}}\mcl K)&\mcl D}
\preceq0.
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
For analysis, $\mcl K$ is fixed and
\eqref{eq:primal-controller-analysis} is affine in the certificate operators
$\mcl P$ and $\mcl V$. For synthesis, these operators and $\mcl K$ must be
determined simultaneously. This introduces bilinearities between $\mcl P$ and
$\mcl K$, and between $\mcl K$ and $\mcl V$.
For a non-descriptor ODE, the storage and controller coupling can be removed
with $\mcl Q=\mcl P^{-1}$ and $\mcl Z=\mcl K\mcl Q$. The operator $\mcl T$ prevents 
the same cancellation for a general PIE. The
multiplier bilinearity must likewise be resolved by additional structure. Thus
the primal formulation provides a convex test for a fixed controller, but not
a convex synthesis condition. The following sections construct a dual
equivalent of the filtered interconnection from which $K$ can be synthesized
convexly without imposing any restrictions on $\mcl{P}$ and minimal restrictions on $\mcl{V}$.
