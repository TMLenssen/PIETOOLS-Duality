This paper established primal--dual IQC equivalence for PIE systems
through $J$-spectral factorization and the induced $L_2$-gain duality result.
The dual-filter construction gives equivalent strict primal and dual IQC
inequalities and a dual robust-performance certificate. It supports
infinite-dimensional channels and memoryless or dynamic filters.

As an application, the dual robust-performance certificate yields a
convex LPI condition for robust state-feedback synthesis. The resulting
closed-loop interconnection is robustly
stable and achieves the prescribed robust performance. This extends nominal PIE
state-feedback synthesis beyond unstructured small-gain conditions.
The numerical examples illustrate the primal and dual analysis conditions
and the state-feedback synthesis condition. They also show that
computation time increases sharply with filter order, motivating efficient
multiplier selection.

The dual IQC representation also paves the way for robust estimator and
feedforward synthesis, as in the finite-dimensional
setting~\cite{kose2009robust,venkataraman}. Developing an elimination lemma
for PIE systems would allow this approach to be investigated for
infinite-dimensional systems. Further work also includes hard
$J$-spectral factorizations for multipliers acting on infinite-dimensional
channels.
