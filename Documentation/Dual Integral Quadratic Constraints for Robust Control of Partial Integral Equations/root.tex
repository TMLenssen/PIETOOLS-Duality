\title{Dual Integral Quadratic Constraints for Robust Control of Partial Integral Equations}

\author{Thijs Lenssen}
\address{Control Systems Group, Eindhoven University of Technology, The Netherlands}
\ead{t.m.lenssen@student.tue.nl, thijs.lenssen14@gmail.com}

and verified by the author.}

\begin{abstract}
Partial Differential Equations (PDEs) model spatially distributed systems
whose control must account for uncertainty and nonlinearities. Partial
Integral Equations (PIEs) provide an operator representation supporting
convex nominal $H_\infty$ state-feedback synthesis. We extend this approach
to robust control using Integral Quadratic Constraints (IQCs). Synthesis
introduces bilinear couplings between the controller, storage, and
multiplier operators. Combining induced $L_2$-gain duality with hard $J$-spectral
factorization, we establish a dual IQC representation. For an admissible factorization, 
this yields a convex Linear PI Inequality (LPI) that permits 
explicit recovery of a controller with guaranteed closed-loop robustness. The method
accommodates infinite-dimensional uncertainty channels and dynamic filters.
\end{abstract}
