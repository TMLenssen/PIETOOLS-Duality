# Figures

- `Diagrams`: primal/dual and Potapov-Ginzburg TikZ sources used by the paper.
- `LFR`: editable-diagram exports in SVG, PNG and PDF, plus TikZ sources and an index.
- `Examples/Example1`, `Example2`, `Example3`: numerical results and simulation videos.
- `Cart`: rigid/flexible videos, equation panels, and the animation generator.
- `HeatedRod`: schematic, MATLAB source, and feedback figure.
- `STN_GPe`: trajectories, simulations, data, and aligned diagram overlays.
- `Printing`: drying illustration and its source prompt.

Older figure variants are kept in topic-specific `Archive` folders. PDF/PNG pairs
are retained for LaTeX and PowerPoint respectively. Regeneration scripts remain
beside their inputs and outputs. The paper's figure references use these paths.
