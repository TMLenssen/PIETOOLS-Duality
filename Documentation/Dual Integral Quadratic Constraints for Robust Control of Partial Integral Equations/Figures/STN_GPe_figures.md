The synthesis uses `Code/Executables/Examples/Example_4/Example_4_synthesis.m`
with its solver settings unchanged. A generated copy under `build/stn_sector`
adds the disturbance only to the simulated interconnection, by duplicating
the nonlinear-input channels as exogenous inputs. The original source is
not modified. MATLAB, PIETOOLS, and MOSEK are required for synthesis.

The cache `stn_gpe_sector_simulation.mat` contains the controller, synthesis
and independent primal/dual analysis results, simulation data, source
provenance, and spatial-resolution comparisons. The disturbance is
`d(t) = [5;-5]*sin(pi*(t-0.10)/0.04)^2` on `[0.10,0.14]` seconds and zero
elsewhere, injected through `w = Delta(z) + d`. The initial history is
`[x_S;x_G] = [0;0]`. The plotted control is `u_paper = 27.6*u_code`, matching
the paper's `tau_S*x_S_dot = delta_S(z_S)-x_S+u` convention. Consequently,
`u_paper` is an effective firing-rate input in spikes/s. It is not an
electrode current; conversion to amperes requires a calibration for `I_e`.

The admissibility comparison retains the paper's previously reported bounds
0.492 (sector) and 0.562 (slope). The newly synthesized simulation controller
has its own verified bound, 0.4609375, and uses the corresponding sector
limits 105.918846 (STN) and 183.978757 (GPe). Satisfying these limits along a
simulated trajectory does not establish invariance for other histories.
